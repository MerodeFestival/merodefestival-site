The three guitarists

Sam Grassie

Scotland

Fingerstyle guitarist and songwriter, raised in Glasgow and now based in London. The Bert Jansch Foundation called him one of the finest folk guitarists of his generation. On his EP Sandwood (2024), Celtic melodies meet the rhythm of desert blues.

Watch on YouTubeListen on Spotify: Where Two Hawks Fly

Dylan Fowler

Wales

Born in Tripoli, raised in South Wales and devoted to acoustic and classical guitar for forty years. His work links Celtic tradition with European jazz and influences from the Balkans and Scandinavia. He was the driving force behind the Welsh band Alaw.

Watch on YouTubeListen on Spotify: A Passionate Landscape

Haroun Iqbal

Flanders

Guitarist, composer and multi-instrumentalist. His path led from jazz and classical music to West African kora music and finally to Indian raga. In 2025 the Flemish radio station Klara selected him for De Twintigers.

Watch on YouTubeListen on Spotify: Iqnovara

Every evening follows the same pattern: three half-hour solo sets, then 25 minutes together. You hear three very different guitar worlds, and what happens when they meet.
