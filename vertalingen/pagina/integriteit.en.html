Integrity contact person

Everyone should feel safe and respected at our concerts, rehearsals and tours. Has something gone wrong, or are you unsure? Talk to our integrity contact person.

Jolan Huygens is your contact person

At WESP vzw and Merodefestival you can turn to Jolan Huygens. Jolan listens, treats what you share in confidence and works out with you what you need.

Email Jolan: jolan@merodefestival.be

Would you rather talk first? Ask for a conversation or a phone call in your email, at a time and place that feel right for you. Your message reaches Jolan only, not the rest of the team.

Not a chatbot, but a person. Behind this address there is no clever software and no automatic reply, but someone who actually reads, writes back and drinks coffee. And deliberately someone from outside our day-to-day work: Jolan doesn’t book concerts, doesn’t sit at the ticket desk and has nobody to protect. So you can speak freely.

When can you turn to Jolan?

For anything that crosses a line or gives you a bad feeling, big or small, recent or long ago. For example:

  • unwanted touching, comments or messages, sexual jokes or advances;
  • bullying, gossip, exclusion or ridicule;
  • discrimination or racism, because of who you are, where you come from or who you love;
  • abuse of power: pressure, intimidation, unreasonable demands or agreements that are not kept;
  • aggression or violence, verbal or physical;
  • something you witnessed and can’t let go of, even if it wasn’t about you.

The contact person is there for everyone who works with us or comes to see us: musicians and technicians, volunteers, staff, partners and organisers, and the audience.

How do we work?

A small, clear method in five steps. You stay in control: nothing happens without your consent.

1. You get in touch

By email or in person, with as much or as little detail as you like. You can also ask a question anonymously or just test the waters first.

2. Jolan listens

Within five working days you get a reply and a proposal for a conversation. Jolan doesn’t judge and doesn’t interrogate you: it’s about your story.

3. Weighing it up together

What do you need? A listening ear, advice, a conversation with the person involved, someone to mediate, or a formal report to the board. You choose.

4. Action

Jolan does what was agreed, or refers you to specialised help. If someone is in immediate danger, we step in straight away and tell you so honestly.

5. Follow-up

After a few weeks Jolan asks how you are and whether the agreements are working. We only close the matter when you are happy with it.

Confidential

  • What you share stays with Jolan. Names and details only go further if you want them to.
  • The only exception: if someone is in serious and imminent danger. Even then, we first discuss with you what we will do.
  • Jolan only keeps what is needed to help you properly, stores it safely and deletes it when it is no longer needed.
  • Anyone who reports something must never suffer any disadvantage from doing so.

What do we do with it as an organisation?

Once a year Jolan informs the board of WESP vzw: how many questions and reports there were, about what kind of situations, and what we can improve. Always without names or details that could identify anyone. That way we learn and prevent it from happening again, for example with clear agreements at concerts and on tour.

Would you rather talk to someone outside WESP?

That is always possible, especially if it concerns someone in our team or on the board.

  • Mores.online: independent reporting point for inappropriate behaviour in the cultural sector.
  • 1712: free and anonymous helpline for violence, abuse and child abuse (in Dutch). Call 1712.
  • In an emergency: call 112, or the police on 101.
