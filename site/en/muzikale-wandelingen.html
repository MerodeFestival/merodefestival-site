Walking with live music

Musical walks

With acoustic musicians from folk and world music

A walk through woods, heath or village, with short acoustic performances along the way. At your place or in the Merode, fully organised by us. Also as an outing for your team or your clients.

Request a musical walk

The concept

A musical walk brings walkers and musicians together in a landscape. You walk a few kilometres through woodland, heath, village or heritage site, and along the way you hear short acoustic performances: beside a stream, at a chapel, on a hill.

Musical walks have been part of Merodefestival since the first edition in 2015. We have learned what works: small groups, acoustic music and respect for the surroundings.

How it works

1. Choose a walk.

  • 3 km with 2 artists
  • 5 km with 3 artists
  • a festival walk of 7 to 8 km with four musical stops

2. Choose a starting point. Together we find the ideal starting point, taking your practical requirements into account.

3. Enjoy. We take care of the artists and the organisation: route, parking, catering and liaising with landowners.

Done before

In 2025 the walks passed through Westmeerbeek, Langdorp, Tongerlo and Veerle-Heide. For ‘Ode aan de Merode’ we created eight walks in the Landscape Park, and in our walking podcast folk musicians take you along on their way.

Musical walks is a concept by Merodefestival (WESP vzw), with the support of the Flemish Government, arts & heritage.

Practical info

Where
At a place of your choice: forest, heath, village or heritage site
Format
A walk of 3 to 8 kilometres with 2 to 4 live performances along the way, for up to 30 participants
Line-up
2 to 4 acoustic musicians or groups, chosen in consultation
Technical
Acoustic only, with respect for the surroundings
Suitable for
Companies, municipalities and tourist offices, heritage sites and nature estates
Price
On request
