Playlist, 2025

Derroll 100

Eight songwriters on Derroll Adams, produced by Laura Cortese

On 27 November 2025, banjo man Derroll Adams would have turned one hundred. Laura Cortese asked eight songwriters from Europe and North America each to write a new song inspired by his life and songs, from Meskerem Mees and Kaia Kater to Rowan Rheingans and Johannes Wannyn.

The Sound of the Abbey

Thirteen artists and ensembles in residence at the Abdij van Park

Three years of musical residencies at Abdij van Park, just outside Leuven: thirteen artists and ensembles each created a piece for a spot in the abbey, from the library and the cloister to the upper choir, and recorded it on location.

Ode aan de Merode

Nine artists and ensembles in residence in the Merode

Nine artists, duos and ensembles from the Flemish folk and world music scene went on residency in the Merode. Their journey of discovery through nature and heritage produced fifteen new pieces, each one an ode to the Merode.

Merodefestival, with Lennaert Maes, Jochen Smets and Bert Huysentruyt

The traditional pubs of the Merode

Merodefestival, with singer-songwriter Lennaert Maes

Singer-songwriter Lennaert Maes visits three old traditional pubs in the Merode and writes a song for each landlord: three moving portraits of around 25 minutes each.
