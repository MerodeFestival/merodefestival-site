Work with us

For municipalities, church councils and heritage sites

Do you have a chapel, a church or a heritage site that calls for music? We bring folk and world music there. We choose the musicians and take care of equipment and organisation, and you enjoy it together with your residents. For one evening, a series or a full festival day.

Formats for your venue

What we do at our own venues, we also bring to your town, church or heritage site. We take care of the programme, the technical side and the hosting; you bring a space that calls for music.

  • Solo in a chapelKlanken in de kapelWith solo musicians from folk, world music, jazz and classicalOne musician, a chapel and a small audience: a short acoustic evening where music and silence meet. We take care of everything, from liaising with the chapel to coordination on the day itself.Discover the format →
  • Walking with live musicMusical walksWith acoustic musicians from folk and world musicA walk through woods, heath or village, with short acoustic performances along the way. At your place or in the Merode, fully organised by us. Also as an outing for your team or your clients.Discover the format →
  • A second evening in your churchSecond dates for our church concertsWith the artists of our church concertsThe same artists and the same programme as our church concerts, on another evening in a church in your municipality. We come with team and equipment; you bring the audience.Discover the format →
  • A festival day at your heritage siteA festival day at a heritage siteWith folk and world musiciansA heritage site, a day and a programme that suits the place. We build a festival day around your site, as we do at the Abdij van Park and the Citadel of Diest. We take care of the programme and the organisation; you provide the venue and the audience in your municipality.Discover the format →
