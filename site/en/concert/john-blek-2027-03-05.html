John Blek (irl)

Buurthuis, Holsbeek

Buy tickets (€ 16)

John Blek is an Irish singer-songwriter from Cork. He writes poetic songs rooted in the Irish, English and American traditions, which he performs solo with his beautiful voice and virtuoso guitar playing. Fragile and understated, yet powerful and melodic.
