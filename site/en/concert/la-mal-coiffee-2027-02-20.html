Saturday 20 February 2027 · 8 pm

A storm of voices from the south of France

La Mal Coiffée (fr)

Sint-Pieterskerk, Langdorp

Buy tickets (€ 22)

Four female voices from the Minervois, together for more than twenty years. La Mal Coiffée sings exclusively in Occitan and builds its music on two pillars: polyphonic singing and percussion on traditional drums. No electric instruments, no artificial layer — just voice and rhythm.

Their latest albums Roge and Rojas deal with resistance, disobedience and the people's history of the Languedoc; the French press described their music as "un coup de gueule" — literally a cry from the throat. This is not nostalgic folklore, but a living language that refuses to disappear.

"La Mal Coiffée speaks for all oppressed cultures. Powerful." — Anne Berthod, Télérama
