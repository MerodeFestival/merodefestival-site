Thursday 26 November 2026 · 8 pm

Lenny & de Wespen

Buurthuis, Holsbeek

Buy tickets (€ 16)

Lenny & de Wespen present their new album Noem het dan maar dromen, a collection of songs where folk, pop, Americana and Dutch-language songwriting meet. The music is restrained and melodic, often built around fingerpicked guitar and strong, poetic lyrics, breathing both light and melancholy. With a warm, acoustic approach, the trio creates an intimate atmosphere.

With Lennaert Maes (vocals, guitar), Andries Boone (mandolin, guitar) and Bert Huysentruyt (drums, bass)
