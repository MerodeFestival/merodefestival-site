Eriksson Delcroix

Buurthuis Nieuwrode, Holsbeek

Buy tickets

Eriksson-Delcroix is the country and bluegrass duo of Bjorn Eriksson and Nathalie Delcroix. With two-part harmonies and guitars they move through Americana and folk, from their own songs to roots music classics. Close harmony and virtuoso guitar playing are the duo's hallmarks.

With Nathalie Delcroix (vocals) and Bjorn Eriksson (vocals, guitar)
