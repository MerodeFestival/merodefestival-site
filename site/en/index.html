  25. 17Apr 2027

    From Italy to Greece

    Livia Giaffreda & Nino Conte, Katerina Douka & Stamatis Pasopoulos (ita-gr)

    Merodefestival church concert · Sint-Pieterskerk, Langdorp · 8 pm

    Short description

    Livia Giaffreda is a singer and percussionist focusing on Italian and Mediterranean traditions. She works with voice and frame drum, performing both traditional songs and her own interpretations. Her repertoire starts from oral traditions and is given a contemporary twist, supported by accordionist Simone Frezza.

    Katerina Douka & Stamatis Pasopoulos present a programme of Greek repertoire for voice and accordion. At its heart is rebetiko, the urban folk music that emerged in the ports of the Greek peninsula. Themes such as migration, loss and longing resonate through this music. The duo approaches this repertoire with respect for tradition and with attention to lyrics, melody and interplay.

  28. 19Jun 2027

    New sound colours

    Sara Salvérius & blazerskwartet: Adem

    Merodefestival church concert · O.L.V. Onbevlekt Ontvangen-kerk, Blauberg · 8 pm

    Short description

    With Adem, Sara Salvérius brings her accordion together with a wind quartet, each musician with their own background and sound world. The project starts from shared breathing as a source of sound and musical dialogue.

    Together they explore the wind that rushes through their instruments, sometimes in the wistful tone of a whispered last word, sometimes with the exuberance of a brass band. Between composition and improvisation they celebrate life, farewell and death, in a search for the cadence that gives every life its rhythm. Because everything is breath.
