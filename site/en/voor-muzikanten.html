For musicians

A collaboration, not a booking

You have an idea, we have the places: abbeys, chapels, a music museum and the Merode landscape. You spend a week there in residency, play the premiere on one of our stages and then play on, with us and elsewhere. It all starts with a conversation, not a contract.

From residency to premiere, and beyond

  1. A conversation

    You tell us what you want to make. We find the place that fits.

  2. A week’s residency

    In an abbey, a museum or the landscape, with accommodation and technical support.

  3. The premiere

    At one of our festivals or series, recorded in sound and image, with programmers in the audience.

  4. Playing on

    With us and elsewhere. If you like, we book your creation too.
