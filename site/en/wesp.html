About WESP

WESP is the organisation behind Merodefestival.

WESP brings music to places that call for it

… and never lets go of either. An abandoned abbey, a fenland where you can hear the frogs, a church with acoustics no concert hall can give you.

We organise events at heritage and nature sites

On our own initiative, or commissioned by municipalities or venues: Merodefestival, church concerts, summer programming in Leuven, musical walks.

Go to What's on →

We commission new work for our venues

Residencies in abbeys, a museum and the landscape, with a premiere on one of our own stages. The musicians remain the makers and authors; we present the work and record it.

For musicians →

We tell the story of the folk and world music scene

Through video, recordings, books or podcasts we capture what happens in those special places, so that it doesn't end with the final applause.

Watch and listen →

The Merode method

For us, the landscape is never just a backdrop. An abbey, a village church or a marshy woodland is a deliberate artistic choice that helps shape how music sounds, what it tells and who comes to listen.

Music
acoustic, melodic, storytelling
Landscape
walks, nature reserves, listening spots
Heritage
abbeys, churches, chapels, traditional pubs
Community
church councils, municipalities, 40 volunteers

Klanken van de Steden

The musical culture of migrant communities in Antwerp and Brussels, with residencies, concerts, portraits and walks. Together with Muziekpublique, MIM and Muziekmuseum Vleeshuis.
